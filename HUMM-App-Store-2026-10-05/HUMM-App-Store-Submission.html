Review notes — copy after completing store/device acceptance
HUMM is a music creation app. Microphone analysis and synthesis run locally; no recording or song is uploaded to an AI or music-processing service. No app account or sign-in is required or offered.

To try the main flow, tap the Home microphone and hum or sing an original melody for at least 10 seconds. Stop, choose one of the first three arrangements, and use it. Tap the playing arrangement again to pause. Add parts in Studio. Turn the phone sideways to use Tracks. Select a 2-, 4- or 8-bar set; arrow buttons move between sets, + appends a set, and − removes the final set with Undo. Tracks supports arrangements up to five minutes; the Home mic remains 10–60 seconds.

The first three generated versions can export free as WAV or watermarked video, including their automatically assigned sounds. Extra versions, manually picked premium sounds and the voice effect need Plus or Pro to export. Tracks/Fix edits and MIDI need Pro. Paid tools can be previewed before subscribing. Monthly products are humm_plus_monthly (Plus) and humm_pro_monthly (Pro), in one subscription group. The intended US base prices are $1.99 and $4.99 respectively; the app uses the actual localized store prices.
