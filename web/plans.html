GOOD TO KNOW.

Do I need to pay to try HUMM?

No. Start with Free, keep songs on your device and share the first three arrangements of each hum. You can also try paid editing tools before deciding whether to subscribe to export those edits.

What about the three-month Pro trial?

A three-month free Pro introductory offer is planned for launch, subject to App Store availability and eligibility. The app will show your exact offer, renewal price and terms before you confirm. It is not available on this website yet.

Are Plus and Pro monthly subscriptions?

Yes. The planned US prices are $1.99 per month for Plus and $4.99 per month for Pro. Pro includes Plus. Purchases and renewal terms will be shown by the App Store; manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple account settings.

Does the five-minute limit change the microphone?

No. The Home microphone records 10 seconds to one minute on every plan. Five minutes is the maximum arrangement length you can build in Tracks. Studio recordings and imports have the separate plan limits listed above.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No HUMM account is needed. Recording and music processing happen on your device. The App Store and purchase verification need internet access. Keep exported copies of ideas you want to back up.
