Your melody comes first

Hum, sing or whistle for 10–60 seconds. Keep your original voice alongside the instruments.

A band you can shape

Hear three starting versions, then change the instruments, balance each part, or beatbox a drum pattern. The first set includes a version close to your original melody and tempo.

Room to finish the song

Edit notes and drum hits in Tracks. Add matching sets of bars to build an arrangement up to five minutes. Pro includes exports using Tracks and Fix.

MAKE A LITTLE.
TAKE IT FURTHER.

Choose how deep you want to go.

A sound for your idea

Explore 20 styles, 37 instruments and 16 drum kits. Listen before choosing a sound. Plus includes exports with premium sounds you select and extra versions from More.

Your voice belongs in the mix

Keep the original take, tune it, or add a voice effect. Balance your voice against the band. Plus includes voice effects in the songs you export.

Small edits. Bigger songs.

Adjust notes, fix timing and build longer arrangements in Tracks. Preview the tools freely; Pro is required to export songs that use Tracks or Fix.

A place for every idea

Auto-save keeps your projects on your device. Rename, duplicate, favorite and revisit songs, with recently deleted ideas available to recover.
