FOR THE MELODY
YOU ALREADY HAVE.

That small tune you keep humming deserves somewhere to go.

HUMM listens to the notes and rhythm you bring, then builds instrumental parts around them. Keep your recorded voice in the mix, choose the sounds and make the idea your own.

You can stay with a quick sketch, or take the notes further. Your recordings are processed locally on your phone, with no HUMM account to set up.
