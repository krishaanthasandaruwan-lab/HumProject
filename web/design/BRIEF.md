Audience: people with a melody in their head. Job: show how HUMM turns sound into music, let visitors try it, and lead to the iPhone app. Direction: a playful, minimal recording studio, using the app's exact paper/ink/Stage Red system. Signature: a playable upside-down piano attached to the full-width footer.

Two-pixel borders, square action buttons, hard black offset shadows. No gradients or unrelated colors. Display sizes scale with the viewport; body is at least 16px. Desktop content width is 1440px, with 48–72px gutters; mobile gutters are 20px.
